About Scarlet Roasters

site built by Gigachad Ashik Besant,33:136:440, Fall 2026.

A five-person coffee roaster in New Brunswick, New Jersey. About 97% of what we roast goes to wholesale customers — cafés, restaurants and grocers — and the rest to our web shop.

We buy green coffee from three importers, roast on a weekly schedule, and pack in 12 oz, 2 lb and 5 lb bags. Every wholesale order is confirmed against stock and credit before it is picked, and every shipment leaves with a packing slip and, for wholesale accounts, an invoice on net-30 terms.
